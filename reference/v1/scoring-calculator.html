How to read the calculation

  1. Capability score = Σ(level × question weight) / Σ(question weight).
  2. Pillar score = Σ(capability_score × capability weight) / Σ(weight); only answered capabilities are included.
  3. Overall = direct SUMPRODUCT over all capabilities (not the mean of the pillars).
  4. Gap = target − current; Priority score = capability_weight × gap.
  5. Threshold: <25 questions = blocked · 25-39 = warning · ≥40 = ok.
